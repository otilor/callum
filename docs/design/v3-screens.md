Copy the head block (support.js, Onest, base CSS, reduced-motion block), the header (callum wordmark with sun dot, status pill), the marble() avatar helper, toasts, panels and animation classes from those boards. Same people and companies as V2Morning (Kemi Eze at Oko Pay, Femi at Eko Wallet, Zainab at Lagoon Pay, Chidi, Bisi at Ikoyi Pay, Ngozi, Tunde at Ireti Labs, Emeka at Surulere Labs, Seun at Lekki Ledger, Ada, Nneka, Temi, Ruth, Tomás, Amara, Mei, Sam Bello). The user is Gabriel, sending from gabriel@yourcompany.com. Offer line: "You help fintech teams take reconciliation off their ops team's plate."

Rules for every board: light mode; white ground; ink #1D2140, slate #646B85, mist #F4F5F9, line #E7E9F0; sun #FFB020 / tint #FFF3D6 only for people who replied and Callum's voice; green #22B07D; error tint #FFF1EC with icon #D9480F; stage rings #D5D9E4 #A9B8F5 #6E86F2 #3F52D9; Onest. Low text, seamless, details open in place, faces instead of rows, no dashboards, no charts, no percentages, no ALL-CAPS labels, no exclamation marks, exact times ("Thursday at 9:00"). Words: people, hello, follow-up, talking, resting, pause, Callum (never leads, contacts, touch, cadence, engaged, disable, "the AI"). Every error says what happened, what Callum already did, and the one thing to do. Every unavailable action says why beside it. Each board is interactive, works at 1440 wide (phone boards 390x844), has a 760px media query and a reduced-motion block, and passes the smoke harness (node smoke-generic.js <file> <steps.js>): no missing holes, no NaN/undefined, loops ok, all timers cleared on unmount.

## Boards

1. V3Settings.dc.html — "Your Callum" (1440x900). Not a settings dashboard: one calm column of rows that open in place.
   Rows: You (Gabriel, sending as gabriel@yourcompany.com); What you offer (editable one line); How you sound (Warm / Direct / Playful chips, sample rewrites in place, "Callum learns from every edit you make"); Calendar link (field, "Used when you tap Book a call", saves with a check; empty state "Add one so Book a call works"); Where Callum reaches people (email, LinkedIn, X rows: status dot, account, today's pace in words: "20 a day this week, 120 by week 3", "15 invites a day, runs on this computer while Callum is open", "20 messages a day"; Reconnect / Disconnect with a confirm in place: "Disconnect LinkedIn? 6 invites and 2 follow-ups wait until you connect again."); When Callum sends (read-only explanation: "9:00 to 17:00 their time, Monday to Friday. Callum never sends on your behalf at night."); Taps (replies only toggle, morning brief at 8:30 toggle); Your data (Download everything, Take a break or wrap up). Interactive: open/close rows, edit offer, tone switch rewrites sample, calendar save, disconnect confirm and cancel, toggles.

2. V3Connect.dc.html — "Connect a channel" (1440x900). A sheet with three tabs: Email, LinkedIn, X.
   Email: Google / Microsoft / Another provider. Google path explains the app password in three short steps (Turn on 2-step verification, Create an app password, Paste it here) with "Open Google" link; a 16-letter field; Connect -> ring spins -> check and "Sending from gabriel@yourcompany.com. 20 a day this week, 120 by week 3." Error: "Google didn't accept that password. App passwords are 16 letters, with no spaces." with the field highlighted. Personal Gmail warning: "This looks like a personal address. Cold email works best from your company address."
   LinkedIn: "Callum runs LinkedIn on this computer while Callum is open. You sign in on LinkedIn's own page. Callum never sees your password." Open LinkedIn -> waiting "Finish signing in on the LinkedIn window" with Reopen -> connected "Connected as Gabriel. 15 invites a day, working hours only. Callum pauses itself if LinkedIn asks anything." Disclosure "Running Callum on a server?" -> paste session cookie field with a one-line how-to.
   X: "X messages go through X's official API, which needs a paid X plan." Steps (create an app at developer.x.com, copy the access token) and field; connected "@gabriel. 20 messages a day."; error for a free-plan token: "This X plan can't send messages. Basic or higher is needed."
   Interactive: tabs, provider choice, connect success and error, LinkedIn waiting/reopen/connected, cookie disclosure.

3. V3Person.dc.html — "A person" (1440x900). Opens from a face or a list row. Search field at the top ("Find someone") filters a small strip of faces.
   Left column: big face with stage ring, name, role, company, channel chip, the path (Hello Tue 9:12, Follow-up Sat, ...), what's next ("Follow-up goes out Thursday at 9:00 by email"), why this channel ("Callum picked email because Kemi's address is verified."), actions Nudge now, Hold / Resume, Move to LinkedIn (with the "never two channels in one week" reason when unavailable: "Callum can switch Kemi to LinkedIn from Monday, a week after the last email."), Never contact (confirm in place).
   Right column: the whole conversation across channels as a calm thread (Callum's messages in white cards, theirs in sun tint, yours in ink outline; channel and exact time on each). Corrections on their last message: That's an auto-reply / Wrong person.
   People to switch between: Kemi (talking, booked Thursday 14:00), Chidi (following up on LinkedIn, accepted), Ruth (stopped: "Ruth asked not to be contacted. Callum stopped everything and won't reach out again."), Mei (resting until 3 February).

4. V3AddPeople.dc.html — "Add people" (1440x900), after first run, from the Ask bar or "Add people". Ways: Drop a CSV, Paste names, LinkedIn search link, Describe who.
   CSV path: drop -> "Reading founders.csv" with a live count -> preview: faces of new people pouring into the strangers lane, "41 new people", and skipped people grouped with reasons you can open: "3 you've emailed before", "2 already in Callum", "1 asked not to be contacted", "1 has no email or LinkedIn". Add 41 -> "41 people join the river. First hellos from tomorrow at 9:00." Undo.
   Describe path: type "heads of finance at fintechs in Accra" -> "That's about 2,300 people. Narrow it down?" with chips (Seed to Series A, 20 to 200 people, Accra only) -> "Callum found 20" peek grid with Not a fit -> Add 20. Nobody found state: "Nobody matched 'heads of ops in Accra'. Try a wider role or a nearby city." with chips.
   No source connected state for Describe: "Describing people needs a people search connected. Bring a list instead, or connect Apollo in Your Callum."

5. V3Breaks.dc.html — "When something else breaks" (1440x900). A row of situations to flip through, each showing the river behind and one incident card in the V2Paused style (what happened, what Callum did, the one thing to do):
   Mailbox signed out ("Google signed Callum out of gabriel@yourcompany.com. 14 emails are waiting and nothing was lost." Reconnect Google -> check -> "14 emails go out from 9:00 tomorrow"); Emails bounced ("8 addresses at fieldnote.com bounced. Callum removed them and will try LinkedIn for those people." Undo, to skip them instead); X limit ("X stopped accepting Callum's messages because your X plan's daily limit is reached. DMs resume tomorrow at 9:00." Okay / Raise the X plan); Reply didn't go out ("Your reply to Tomás didn't go out: Gmail says the mailbox is full. It's saved here." Try again / Copy); LinkedIn weekly limit ("LinkedIn's weekly invite limit is reached. New invites wait until Monday. Messages to your connections keep going." Move these 6 to email); Someone said stop (a quiet toast-like card: "Ruth asked not to be contacted. Callum stopped everything and won't reach out again.").
   The affected faces in the river react (email lane faces wait with a small clock, bounced faces fade, X faces still).

6. V3Quiet.dc.html — "Quiet moments" (1440x900). Flip through edge states on the home screen:
   Empty river ("Your river is empty. Tell Callum who you want to meet." cursor blinking in the Ask bar); Day two, no replies ("No replies yet, which is normal this early. Callum will tap you the moment someone answers." plus "Next: 12 hellos tomorrow at 9:00" and a peek of what went out); Group used up ("Everyone in this group has heard from you." + "Find more like the people who replied"); Offline (a slim top line "You're offline. Callum keeps working, and your changes sync when you're back."); Out of office (Kemi's face with a small sun-down badge: "Kemi is away until 20 November. Callum will wait and follow up on the 21st."); Referral (Sam Bello joins as a warm intro with the context "Kemi said Sam runs finance at Oko Pay" and a draft that mentions Kemi); Paused for three days reminder ("Callum has been paused for three days. Resume, or pick a date?").

7. V3Weekly.dc.html — "The weekly note" (1440x900). Monday 8:30. A note that opens over the river, in people, not percentages: faces of who replied this week, "Callum said hello to 42 people. 6 replied and 2 calls are booked." ; "Warming up" faces (accepted, opened their LinkedIn), "Resting" count with when they come back; one suggestion with an action ("Kemi's colleagues look like a good fit. Find 10 more like Kemi?"). Plus the email version of the same note side by side, as it would land in Gmail (same content, plain). Interactive: open/close, act on the suggestion, see the email version.

8. V3Phone.dc.html — "Phone, the rest" (390x844). Bottom bar with three places: River, Ask, You. River: compact river strip + list of people by stage, tap -> person sheet (conversation + Nudge / Hold). Ask: the Ask bar with chips and an answer card. You: compact Your Callum (channels, pace in words, take a break). Lock screen state first: the 8:30 morning brief notification ("3 people want to talk. Kemi asked about Thursday.") that opens the app.

9. V3SignIn.dc.html — "Sign in" (1440x900), for a Callum hosted on a server with a password. "This Callum is private. Enter the password you set when you installed it." Field, Sign in, wrong password ("That's not the password. Ask whoever set up this Callum, or check CALLUM_PASSWORD on the server."), success -> "Callum is waking up" with the river fading in. Also the starting state when the server just booted.
